This stop-motion project presents the story of a pomegranate seed that constantly says “yes” in order to please others, eventually losing its energy and sense of self. Although the seeds appear identical, the protagonist is characterized by its kindness, self-sacrifice, and emotional vulnerability. However, hese traits also make it susceptible to being used by others. As the seed continues to prioritize external expectations over its own needs, it gradually dries out and collapses, symbolizing emotional burnout and the consequences of lacking personal boundaries.
